Start practising How it works

You understand more than you can say.

You follow the meeting. You get the joke a second late. The word you needed shows up on the way home. Reading more won't close that gap. Building sentences yourself, again and again, until they come out on their own, will.

Ten minutes, three moves.

  1. Build

    You see a sentence in English and put the German together. Tap the words into place, drag them to reorder, or type it out. Your hands help your memory.

  2. Check

    Wrong order? The words that are off turn red. Move them and try again. Fixing it yourself is what makes it stick.

  3. Keep

    Sentences you miss come back sooner, the ones you know wait longer. Tap any word you want to keep and it goes into your own list.

What's inside

A new phrase every morning at 08:00

Open the app and a German phrase is waiting, with a short note for the day. Yesterday's stay in your collection.

74 grammar topics, A1 to C2

Plain explanations, real examples, common mistakes. Mark what you know, then practise sentences from exactly the topics you pick.

Tap a word, keep it

Any German word in the app can go into My words, together with the sentence you found it in.

See what you actually use

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No subscription, no paywall, no ads. The project is open source under the MIT license.

Which levels does it cover?

Grammar goes from A1 to C2. Practice sentences are written for learners who already reached B1 or higher and want to speak more naturally. You pick any mix of levels.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser on a phone or a computer. An Android app is available too.

How does the daily phrase work?

Every morning at 08:00 your time, a new German phrase unlocks. Open the app any time after that and it's yours. Earlier phrases stay in your collection.

Which languages is the app in?

The app is in English and Azerbaijani. Translations of the German sentences are in English for now; more languages are planned.
